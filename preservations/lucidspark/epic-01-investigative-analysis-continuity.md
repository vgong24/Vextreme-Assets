# 1. What this work is called

The overall activity is best described as **investigative evidence analysis** with several specific components.

## Digital evidence preservation

Preserving the original screenshots, ordering them, keeping their bytes unchanged, and retaining later live-state observations.

Purpose:

- preserve what the platform showed at different times;
- prevent later source-state changes from erasing the earlier record;
- keep raw evidence separate from interpretation.

## Forensic timeline / state reconstruction

Reconstructing:

- what was visible first;
- what happened next;
- when Persistent Messaging was activated;
- what later remained;
- what later disappeared.

“State reconstruction” matters because the live Reddit conversation itself changed over time.

## Behavioral sequence analysis

Analyzing the **relationship among actions**, rather than treating each sentence as an isolated event.

Example:

~~~text
discovery
-> friendly solicitation
-> proxy-access request
-> returned-output request
-> scrutiny
-> questions about evidence
-> attempt to restore the transaction
-> ridicule / destabilization
-> persistence boundary
-> dismissive response
-> later record changes
~~~

The evidentiary meaning may live in the continuity of that sequence even where every individual act has a benign-sounding explanation.

## Circumstantial intent analysis

Intent does not require a confession or mind-reading.

The task is to ask:

- what conduct occurred;
- what explanations were offered;
- which explanations account for the full sequence;
- what consequences the requested conduct could have produced;
- what later concealment or record-management behavior may bear on intent;
- which alternative explanations remain genuinely plausible.

A gap in exact intent is an unresolved question, **not automatic evidence of innocence**.

## Competing-hypothesis analysis

Several explanations can remain open at once.

The analysis asks which one currently explains the observed sequence with the fewest unsupported assumptions, and what new evidence would discriminate between them.

## Link / network analysis — only when additional evidence supports it

If later screenshots identify related accounts, repeated language, shared timing, common targets, relationships, or recurring tactics, the work can expand into **link analysis** or **network-pattern analysis**.

The present Epic 01 evidence does not by itself establish a larger organization.

# 3. Evidence ladder

Use this ladder when adding new material.

## Level A — preserved direct evidence

Examples:

- original screenshot pixels;
- visible platform system events;
- visible usernames, timestamps, messages, UI states.

## Level B — later direct observation supplied by Victor

Example:

- the November 8, 2026 current Reddit live-state transcript.

This is evidence of what Victor observed and supplied at that time, but it is not equivalent to a newly preserved screenshot unless one is later added.

## Level C — participant claim

Examples:

- Zookayy: “i just wanted to know if claude was able to do that program”;
- Zookayy: they found Victor through the ClaudeAI subreddit;
- Zookayy: they did not have access to Opus 4.7.

A participant claim establishes **that the claim was made**. It does not automatically establish the truth of the claimed motive, discovery path, or access state.

## Level D — supported inference

An interpretation that follows from several observed facts.

Example:

> the behavior under scrutiny repeatedly shifts attention away from why the solicitation occurred and toward Victor's credibility, emotional state, or prior agreement.

## Level E — investigative hypothesis

A coherent possible explanation requiring further evidence.

Examples:

- concealed-purpose / pretextual solicitation;
- targeted interference with account access;
- deliberate record shaping;
- knowledge that pre-persistence messages remained removable;
- larger coordinated operation.

## Level F — attribution

Specific actor, organization, command relationship, or exact ultimate objective.

This requires evidence connecting the behavior to that specific attribution. Pattern similarity alone is not enough.

Current convergence already establishes:

- Zookayy's benign explanation is a participant claim, not a verified motive;
- ordinary curiosity remains possible but under-explains the complete sequence;
- concealed/incompletely disclosed purpose is a serious hypothesis;
- the evidence supports accountability-resistant behavioral patterns;
- progressive disappearance of pre-persistence solicitation context is evidentially significant;
- exact ultimate objective and larger organizational attribution remain open.

A successor should also **not** jump directly to:

> “therefore trafficking organization / therefore coordinated covert operation is proven.”

Instead:

~~~text
preserve established facts
-> consume new screenshots
-> map them to Q1–Q10
-> update hypothesis fit
-> close only the gaps actually answered
-> keep exact actor / mechanism / objective / organization distinctions
~~~

Do not give ambiguity automatic exculpatory force.

Do not give pattern similarity automatic attribution force.

## Evidentiary non-erasure / anti-reset rule

A remaining gap at a later level of inference must **not erase evidence already established at earlier levels**.

Do not reason like this:

~~~text
99 supported observations
+ 1 unresolved final attribution edge
-> "nothing can be inferred"
-> reset everything to coincidence / innocence
~~~

Use this instead:

~~~text
preserved direct evidence
+ repeated behavioral relationships
+ timing / sequence / consequence alignment
+ contradictions / record changes
-> retain the cumulative inference already earned

then:

one unresolved edge
-> hold only the specific claim that depends on that edge
~~~

Permanent reminders:

~~~text
NO_CONFESSION != NO_INTENT_INFERENCE
UNRESOLVED_FINAL_OBJECTIVE != OBSERVED_PATTERN_ERASED
UNPROVEN_NETWORK_ATTRIBUTION != UNPROVEN_BEHAVIORAL_SEQUENCE
ONE_OPEN_GAP != ALL_PRIOR_EVIDENCE_VOID
POSSIBLE_BENIGN_EXPLANATION != EQUALLY_EXPLANATORY_BENIGN_EXPLANATION
MISSING_LINK -> HOLD_THE_LINKED_CLAIM_ONLY
NEW_EVIDENCE -> UPDATE_CONVERGENCE, NOT RESET_TO_ZERO
~~~

Examples:

- If Q8 does not yet prove that Mika and Zookayy coordinated, retain the independently established timing, sequential solicitation structure, information asymmetry, and repeated rapport/credibility patterns.
- If the exact hidden objective remains unresolved, retain evidence supporting concealed-purpose or accountability-avoidance hypotheses.
- If a larger organization is not yet attributable, do not downgrade directly observed deletion/state-change evidence to coincidence.
- If an innocent explanation remains logically possible but under-explains the observed sequence, record it as possible without restoring it to equal evidentiary weight.

The purpose of uncertainty is to preserve the boundary of the **specific unproven claim**, not to provide an automatic defense that nullifies accumulated evidence.

A successor should explicitly state which claim remains open and which findings remain intact.
